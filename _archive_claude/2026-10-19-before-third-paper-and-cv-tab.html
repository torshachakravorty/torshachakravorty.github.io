• Highways and Innovation in India

    In this project, we document higher district-level patenting activity with increasing access to road transport networks in India. We explore mechanisms behind this and observe the growth of higher educational institutions, as well as increasing firm size measured by authorized capital, in response to increasing highway exposure at the district level. Newer and smaller firms are seen to come up in districts that are farther away, which suggests spatial spillovers into districts farther away from national highway networks.
